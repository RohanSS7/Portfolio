Working as a Social Media Marketer for Climate Action

As the new year has begun, one of my New Year's resolutions was to take on more meaningful work that allows me to utilize my primary skills as a marketer for a good cause. This has led me to take on a technical volunteering role as a media marketer for a non-profit organization.

Scottish Communities Climate Action Network

I began searching for volunteering opportunities online, where I stumbled across a social media marketing role on Volunteer Edinburgh, a volunteering job board. Not even a day later, I was immediately contacted through email by one of the fellow members to discuss my role further through a quick Zoom call later that week. Fast forward, and now I was onboarded as a volunteering media marketer for the organization. The organization that I would be and currently am volunteering for is a climate action organization called Scottish Communities Climate Action Network (SCCAN), a volunteer-led network looking to support community-led action in Scotland to address natural emergencies.

The Role

My role initially entailed creating content on Instagram. The primary focus was to ensure that the organisation’s message of addressing climate action and promoting social justice within powerless communities, who tend to be more vulnerable to natural disasters, was effectively communicated. I was operating within the media circle, where I directly reported to the head of the media circle. I would work once a week on a Friday, where my manager would send me resources and reading materials that I would then have to make content about. I had video inductions that taught me some of the key digital marketing metrics and how to utilize META when reading some of the key analytics. This mostly entailed hashtags, engagement, and KPIs. Within my third week, I was then entrusted with creating social media content for their Facebook platform and Vive platform. Some of the content that I have promoted includes roadshows, art exhibitions, fundraisers, and seminars.

My Takeaways

Working as a volunteering media marketer so far has been incredibly meaningful and fulfilling. The idea of making use of your skills for a good cause has brought a profound satisfaction to my days. The role has also taught me how to work on tight schedules and manage deadlines as I balance my media marketing role alongside my part-time job and another volunteering role that I will talk about in my other blog. I have also been able to gain new knowledge through the training resources available from this role. I look forward to growing further in this role, deepening my impact, and continuing to use my creativity in ways that support meaningful, community-led change.
